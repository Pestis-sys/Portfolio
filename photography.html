Photography

Exploring light, texture, and story through the lens. Shot on a Canon R7.

My photography focuses on capturing real moments with cinematic emotion, whether it’s a quiet fog-covered street, a candid expression at a night festival, or the dignity of historical reenactments.

I blend documentary and creative styles: expressive portraits, urban moods, historical storytelling, and peaceful landscapes. Each shot aims to preserve a feeling (stillness, nostalgia, celebration, or resilience) and invites you to see ordinary moments through a more cinematic lens. Select any photo to see it larger.
